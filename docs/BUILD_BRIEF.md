## 2. What the site is

**One-line description (CONFIRMED):** "Real-world threat awareness training paired with the
products that back it up."

**Category (CONFIRMED):** Protection through education and affiliate marketing.

**Core positioning (CONFIRMED):** Sell competence and confidence, not fear. The stated reasoning
(Lance's own words): confidence-based framing converts better for affiliate gear sales because it
positions products as tools of skill rather than fear purchases, and gear recommendations should
read as earned trust, not a storefront.

**Editorial policy — RESOLVED (CONFIRMED 2026-07-29), see §10 item 1 for history.**
Understanding-first, not fear-first: current-events content stays at **trend level only — no
individual victims ever named.** The goal is for visitors to understand threat potential, build
awareness, and see the site as where they come to understand threats and buy/consult their way to
being prepared. Explicitly **not** a fear-based pitch — awareness and solutions, not shock. This
supersedes the earlier "bold and direct, potentially using shock and tragedy" tone note; "bold and
direct" stands as the tone, "shock and tragedy" does not. "Victim-related articles" from the
functionality answer is reinterpreted accordingly: trend/pattern-level security articles, not
individual-victim-focused pieces.

**Revenue model (CONFIRMED, three tracks — see prior conversation for full reasoning, condensed
here):**
1. **Content/affiliate** — current-events-driven threat-awareness content leading into
   product/service reviews with disclosed affiliate/referral links.
2. **Direct consulting** — paid personal/residential/commercial/event security assessments and
   planning engagements, booked from the site.
3. **First-party e-commerce** — an owned "All Out Global" merchandise line (protective gloves,
   ballistic apparel, LEO/security workwear) sold directly, alongside reviewed third-party
   alternatives.

**Content focus (CONFIRMED):** security logistics, personnel, awareness, current events/concerns/
issues, and how to address them.

**Audience (CONFIRMED):** everyone — homeowners, shop owners, pedestrians, passers-by, travelers,
celebrities. Not a narrow B2B or high-net-worth-only pitch; broad general-public reach.

**Primary visitor action (CONFIRMED):** quickly develop interest → learn → purchase. Fast
conversion path is the goal, not a slow trust-building funnel.

## 8. Guardrails (CONFIRMED)

- **No firearm sales on this site.** Firearms content stays in education/training-referral
  territory — do not build any direct firearm purchase/checkout flow.
- **Affiliate disclosure** required, conspicuously, on every page carrying affiliate links —
  agreements not yet in place; do not publish affiliate links before the disclosure component
  exists and the actual affiliate agreements are signed.
- **Body-armor sale restrictions** — some jurisdictions (e.g. NY) require in-person purchase/ID
  verification for body armor and prohibit remote sale. Owned ballistic-apparel checkout needs
  jurisdiction-aware handling before launch, not a generic cart.
- **Protective-rating claims** (e.g. "bulletproof," NIJ ratings) must be accurate and
  substantiated on any product — owned or reviewed — before publishing. False claims here are a
  liability issue, not a copy nit.
